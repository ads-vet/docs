# ACT 2 — WHAT WE LEARNED

## 5 — What we learned in month one

**Claim:** The culture is a superpower — and it's spending itself on manual work we can automate.

**On slide (one strength, three consequences):**
- **The culture is fantastic** — jump in, get it done, ship the issue on time, every time. (Example: Helena hand-reviewed 9,000+ shelter records one by one over ~a month — heroic, and exactly the kind of work we can make faster.)
- **But it manifests as heavy manual work and email** — sourcing, assembling, formatting, coordinating are all by hand, so there's little time for strategic work.
- **On data:** the data is valuable, but customers redo manual matching and cleaning before they can use it.
- **On content:** we have **almost no traffic data** — we can't see which stories resonate. Concretely: click-through to the PDF runs about **1% of opens**, segmentation is a tangle of advertiser-specific lists that don't tell us who's on them, and the product is a PDF with the email as an afterthought. That weakens ad sales and contributor value.

**Visual:** An iceberg or "hours" bar showing how much effort sits below the waterline in manual work; the three consequences as callouts. Honors the team ("this is because you care and hustle"), then points at the fix.

**Design system:** master slide 3 (what we learned).

## PILLAR 1 — DATA (the commercial engine)

### 7 — The data model: access for everyone, upsell as they use it

**Claim:** Every EPD/IPD customer gets the platform; we monetize what they do next.

**On slide:**
- **EPD** = Enterprise Practice Database (~9,700 corporate-owned clinic locations). **IPD** = Independent Practice Database (~19,000 independent practices).
- Every existing EPD/IPD member gets **platform access**. If all they want is the CSV, they still get it — exported from the same platform, and we set up their account so the same report lands on the same schedule.
- Inside the platform they **generate their own reports, build and export lists, and ask questions** (with AI credits included under a generous soft limit) — and we surface upsell as they work.
- **Renewal is the moment**: every renewal call is a platform demo and an upsell conversation.

**Visual:** A funnel/ladder graphic: "CSV (floor)" → "Platform access" → "Self-serve reports & lists" → "Upsell surfaces." Renewal shown as the trigger point on the ladder.

### 8 — The upsell ladder: what we monetize

**Claim:** More data, more frequency, more connections — that's the revenue.

**On slide — the packaging model (from the Jul 13 commercial sync):**
- **Base:** EPD + IPD + **platform access** (login, reports, lists, questions, AI credits). Same anchor price the customer already pays. First push: get IPD-only customers to add EPD and vice-versa.
- **+ More data** — **workforce analysis first** (data about the *people* in every clinic), then new domains: shelters, vendor/tech-stack ("what software do they use"), and later financials.
- **+ More frequency** — increased refresh cadence beyond quarterly (quarterly → monthly → weekly → real-time pulse).
- **+ More connections** — **CRM connectors**, starting with controlled **match-and-return** (customer sends a file → we match, fix, enrich, return it import-ready → they control the import), then a direct sync.
- **Seats, not per-seat (yet):** start every account with a ~10-seat license and sign up all the decision-makers; we push the ceiling on high-value accounts rather than nickel-and-dime small ones.
- **Targets:** ~**$250–260K booked** in the 90 days → on track to **$500K revenue by year-end**, positioned to upsell into 2027.

**Owners:** **Chris** — data partnerships & renewals-led upsell. **Helena + Paul** — the transition and the verification/quality process behind it. **Ben** — platform + connectors.

**Visual:** A three-rung ladder ("+data / +frequency / +connections") climbing to a revenue marker; base rung labeled "EPD + IPD + platform access." Keep dollar figures on one clean marker.

### 11 — The measurement fix: from no signal to click-through → ad sales

**Claim:** We can't sell or serve an audience we can't see — so we're going to see it.

**On slide:**
- **The gap:** ~30,000–40,000 readers, and today we can't say **who they are, who they represent, or what they read.** (Baseline: ~1% of opens click through to the PDF; segments are advertiser-specific and opaque.)
- **The fix:** add **Bitly links** across stories and ads (doable in the next couple of weeks), keep the **PDF for the first 90 days**, and join click-through to **Mailchimp** performance → story-level resonance.
- **Prove the audience is real:** run an incentivized survey to our own list — who's a practicing DVM, is their clinic in our dataset, what's missing — using low-friction mechanics (e.g., a voice-memo "describe your clinic," a "connect your clinic" moment-of-delight). This tests whether our distribution can feed the data business.
- **The payoff:** connect resonant stories to **ad sales** (Connor) and give **contributors** a reason to write for us.
- **Owner:** **Graham** leads understanding the current platform and the audience; **Monica** owns the placement/Mailchimp joins; **Adam** on analytics.

**Visual:** A flow: [30–40K unknown readers] → [Bitly + Mailchimp join] → [story-level click-through chart] → [ad-sales pipeline]. Show a sample resonance bar chart so it reads as concrete.

## 14 — How we work: our automation approach

**Claim:** We automate the same way every time — observe, build, dogfood, introduce, transition.

**On slide (five-step loop):**
1. **Observe & understand** the real workflow.
2. **Build** a narrow agent for the manual part.
3. **Dogfood** it against what we've already done (e.g., the 551-story archive).
4. **Add value & introduce** the people who own the workflow.
5. **Transition off** the manual way.

**Examples on slide:** Chris's **morning meeting-brief** agent · the **Newsfeed Intake Agent** · Mark's **story categorization** · the **account-recap / reconcile skills** built live with Chris on Jul 13 (pull an account's history from email into Notion) · **AI verification agents** that validate conflicting facts (and, ahead, voice agents that call clinics to confirm details / book appointments).
**Principle:** automation removes retrieval, formatting, and admin so people spend time on judgment, taste, and relationships. ("A thousand interns" — start work, check back in 10 minutes.)

**Visual:** A five-step horizontal loop with the real examples pinned beneath the matching step. This is the "we're not hand-waving about AI" slide — make the loop the hero.

**Design system:** master slide 12 (process loop).
