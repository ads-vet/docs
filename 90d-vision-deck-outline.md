# VetIntel Internal Vision Deck — Expanded Slide Outline (v0.3)

**Deck:** Content + Data + New Products — the team vision deck
**Window:** July 10 – October 8, 2026
**Audience:** the full VetIntel / Antelligence operating team
**Drafted:** 2026-07-13, from the 90-Day Operating Plan, Vision & Strategy hub, the Gates v0.2 deck brief (appendix), today's Granola notes from the Helena / Adam / Ben data chat, and this week's live evidence (refresh progress, newsfeed pilot, pipeline motion)

---

## Communication job

By the last slide, every person in the room should be able to:

1. **Repeat the sentence** — say what VetIntel is building in one line.
2. **Place themselves** — point at the pillar and the 30/60/90 row they own.
3. **Feel the momentum** — see that the plan is already moving, not aspirational.
4. **Know the finish line** — name what must be true on October 8 and why it matters.

## Narrative arc

The deck is a story in five beats, not a status report:

**We bought something real → we learned where the value leaks → we're wiring it into one system → here's exactly what the next 90 days prove → here's your part.**

Rules carried over from the v0.2 brief, now enforced per slide: **one claim per slide**, minimal prose, concrete activities, real dates, visible proof. Every slide below states its single claim first — if a slide's content doesn't serve its claim, cut the content, not the claim.

## 4 — What the first month taught us

**Claim:** The value leaks at the seams between the assets — that's exactly what we fix.

**On slide (three learnings, each with its consequence):**
- The **data is valuable, but customers still do too much manual work** — matching, cleaning, moving it into their CRM. → *So we build the platform and the match-and-return workflow.*
- The **content is trusted, but we can't see which stories create value** — no story-level measurement exists. → *So we wire stories to Mailchimp performance and measure everything.*
- The **assets behave like three separate businesses** — data, media, and products don't feed each other yet. → *So the 90-day plan is one loop, not three lanes.*

**Speaker notes:** These came from real renewal calls, the pipeline review, and a month of working inside the systems. This is the "earned insight" slide — it's why the plan looks the way it does.

## 7 — Pillar 1: Data + Platform

**Claim:** July's refresh is the last one delivered as CSV files — from August, customers work in the platform.

**On slide (three time horizons):**

**First 30 days (by Jul 31):**
- Ship the July EPD/IPD refresh — accurate, on time, **no retired fields** (Paul; Ben + Chris sign-off).
- Technical platform load complete and **reconciled against the customer files**.
- Shelter + workforce intelligence models finalized, first ingestion planned.
- Onboarding runbook, permissions, and support path ready.

**Days 30–90:**
- Customer onboarding begins in August; **two customers complete report / list / question end-to-end** without us doing it for them.
- First shelter/workforce ingestion becomes usable data.
- Platform demos join every renewal and prospect conversation.
- First **CRM connector** built, validated, launched.

**Post-90:**
- Faster refresh cycles and continuous enrichment; the platform becomes the normal delivery surface.

**Speaker notes — live proof (as of this week):** Paul reports the refresh on track and posted the first change-stats today (with the honest caveat that most early change is fixing bad data — going forward the deltas mean more). Helena finished the shelter review Friday — **9,000+ records, every one human-reviewed** with an accept / defer / reject / enrich decision — and is already onto the next file (~100 EPD/IPD changes from Paul, walkthrough call tonight). This pillar isn't a plan; it's underway.

**The quality story worth telling here (from today's data chat):** shelters were harder than EPD/IPD because there's no existing database to run automated comparisons against — county records surfaced county names instead of actual shelter names, and many rows had only a name and a phone number. The answer was human judgment on every record, with deferred items preserved for later rather than discarded. That's "trust and provenance are product features" made concrete: our AI does the comparisons where a trusted base exists, and a person makes the call where it doesn't. Helena's review also surfaced that the market is more granular than our categories — shelters vs. sanctuaries (wildlife / farm / reptile), animal control units, breeders, TNR clinics — and each split is a future dataset with a real buyer behind it (Hills nutrition programs, shelter app partners like PetSol). Today's review work is literally tomorrow's product map.

# What changed from the v0.2 brief (and why)

1. **Added slide 2 ("The sentence")** — resolves founder decision #1 into a proposed one-liner and makes it the deck's spine (stated at the open, returned to at the close).
2. **Added slide 3 ("Where we start")** — the v0.2 flow jumped from vision to learnings; the team deck lands harder when it first honors what the team already built (EPD/IPD scale, publication trust, customer book, lineage).
3. **Merged three milestone slides (v0.2 slides 9–11) into one timeline (slide 11)** — company, Vet Together, and KC Corridor milestones are one story ("moments with dates"); three separate slides diluted it.
4. **Added slide 13 ("It's already moving")** — pulls this week's live evidence (refresh stats posted today, shelter list approved, newsfeed pilot running) so the plan reads as underway, not proposed. Designed to be refreshed the night before presenting.
5. **Every slide now leads with its single claim** — v0.2 stated the "one claim per slide" rule; this version enforces it in the outline itself so the deck build can't drift.
6. **Learnings slide (4) now pairs each learning with its consequence** — "we learned X, so we're doing Y" connects diagnosis to plan in one glance.
7. **Sensitivity guardrail added to slide 12** — flags exactly which private-doc numbers appear and confirms the boundary (no runway math, gate colors, or individual goals in the team deck).
