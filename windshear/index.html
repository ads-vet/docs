The new failure modes

You deployed AI in months. Your incident playbook is from 2019.

Every AI system your company adopts creates failure modes no tabletop binder covers — and most organizations have no practical way to find out whether they’re prepared.

WS-01

Deepfake executives

A cloned CFO voice authorizes a wire. Your controls assume voices are real.

WS-02

Agentic AI gone wrong

An autonomous agent makes a bad decision at machine speed, thousands of times.

WS-03

Confidential data leakage

Customer or patient data surfaces in model outputs — quietly, for weeks.

WS-04

Prompt injection

Attackers steer your customer-facing AI through the inputs you invited.

WS-05

Hallucinated communications

Your AI gives customers confident, wrong, screenshot-able answers.

WS-06

AI vendor compromise

The model provider in your critical path becomes the breach vector.

WS-07

AI-generated phishing

Flawless, personalized, at scale. Awareness training built for typos loses.

WS-08

Insider misuse of AI

Employees paste secrets into tools you’ve never inventoried.

WS-09

Regulatory investigation

A regulator asks how your AI made a decision. Someone has to answer.

The annual tabletop can’t keep up. Traditional exercises are expensive, consultant-dependent, generic, and static — a once-a-year event that produces a PDF, not preparedness. The threat surface now changes faster than your exercise calendar.
