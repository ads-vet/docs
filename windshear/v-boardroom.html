SEC. I

The regulatory position

Four regimes now assume your executives can perform under pressure.

Each was written for the file cabinet. Each will be tested in a hallway, at speed, with incomplete information. Policy is the easy half.

SEC cyber disclosureForm 8-K, Item 1.05

A material cybersecurity incident must be disclosed within four business days of the materiality determination. That determination is itself a decision made under pressure — by people who have either rehearsed it or not.3

EU AI ActReg. (EU) 2024/1689

Obligations phase in through 2027: AI literacy for staff, risk management for high-risk systems, deployer duties, serious-incident reporting. Fines are calculated on global turnover. Evidence of a functioning program will matter.4

NIST AI RMFAI 100-1 · Govern / Map / Measure / Manage

The reference framework U.S. regulators, insurers, and counterparties increasingly cite. Its Govern and Manage functions presume the organization tests itself — not merely that it holds documents.5

ISO/IEC 42001AI management systems, 2023

The certifiable AI management standard. Clauses on operation, performance evaluation, and improvement require evidence that the system works in practice and is corrected when it does not.6

The board will ask whether the company could execute under these. Windshear produces the evidence. Not attestations. Observed, scored, dated performance.

Margin note The four-day clock starts at the materiality determination, not at discovery. Delaying the determination is itself a judgment regulators may examine.

SEC. V

Objections, on the record

The four questions general counsel will ask. Answered as counsel would want.

Q.01

Where does our data go, and does it train your models?

Resp.

It stays private, and no. Scenario generation runs on your organizational profile; exercise records remain your records. Client data is not used to train models. Data-handling terms are stated in the agreement, in writing, before the first exercise.

Q.02

Does this become another consulting engagement?

Resp.

No consultants are required. The AI facilitator runs the exercise; your first is 90 minutes, end to end. There is no scoping phase, no workshop pre-read, no day rate.

Q.03

Who sees our scores?

Resp.

You do. Scores are private to your organization. Peer benchmarks are computed from anonymized cohorts; no counterparty can identify, request, or purchase your results.

Q.04

Will the scenarios actually resemble our risk?

Resp.

They are built from it. Scenarios are generated from your industry, your technology stack, and your regulators — the deepfake targets your approval chain, the regulator letter cites your jurisdiction. Generic content is the failure mode Windshear exists to replace.
