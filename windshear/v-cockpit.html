WS/03 Regulatory instrumentation

Your next exercise maps your response to the rules you’ll be judged by.

Regulators no longer ask whether you have a policy. They ask what you did, when, and who decided. Windshear generates that evidence — before the incident that demands it.

REG/SEC

SEC material-incident disclosure

Four business days from a materiality determination to public disclosure. The exercise times how long your team takes to even make the call.

REG/EU

EU AI Act obligations

Obligations attach to deployers of AI systems, not just builders. Scenarios rehearse the documentation and incident duties your tier implies.

FWK/NIST

NIST AI RMF

Exercise evidence is mapped to GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE — showing which functions held under pressure and which are paper.

FWK/ISO

ISO/IEC 42001

The AI management system standard your customers will start asking about. Debriefs flag where rehearsed practice diverges from it.
