# Windshear — concept site

Marketing landing page for the "flight simulator for organizational AI" concept:
an AI-powered platform that generates immersive, organization-specific crisis
simulations, scores the response, and drives continuous readiness improvement.

**Preview:** open any of the HTML files in a browser — each is fully
self-contained (no build, no external assets).

## Files

| File | Direction | The bet |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Original concept page | Balanced baseline (light + dark) |
| `v-cockpit.html` | "Night Ops" — dark instrument-glass, avionics panels, interactive industry scenario selector (Healthcare / Financial Services / SaaS) | Credibility through operational realism; for the hands-on CISO |
| `v-boardroom.html` | "The Board Brief" — governance-journal editorial, serif, footnoted; sample Quarterly Readiness Brief document + NIST AI RMF / EU AI Act / SEC / ISO 42001 evidence-mapping table | Authority; for the CISO selling upward to CFO/GC/board |
| `v-drill.html` | "T-Minus" — opens mid-crisis with a ticking clock and a forced first decision; the page is a taste of the product | Emotional urgency; for the whole executive team |

All variations share the CISO conversion spine: regulatory hooks (SEC 4-day
disclosure, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001), a visible proof artifact
(the board-ready debrief), a low-friction CTA ladder (sample debrief → free
15-min snapshot → 90-min pilot), ROI framing vs. $30–80k consultant tabletops,
and objection handling (client data never trains models, etc.). Real-world
anchors used: the 2024 Arup deepfake fraud (~US$25M) and IBM Cost of a Data
Breach 2025 (~US$4.4M average) — re-verify figures before external publication.
No customer names, testimonials, or certifications are claimed anywhere.

## Why "Windshear"

Windshear is the canonical flight-simulator scenario: sudden, invisible, and
survivable only if the crew has rehearsed it. After windshear training became
mandatory in simulators, windshear accidents in commercial aviation nearly
disappeared — the strongest real-world proof that rehearsal, not documentation,
creates readiness. It's short, ownable, aviation-native, and slightly ominous
in a way CISOs respect.

Suite naming used on the page:

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| Windshear / Scenarios | AI scenario generator |
| Windshear / Storyline | Multi-channel immersive storytelling engine |
| Windshear / Facilitator | AI moderator (replaces consultant-led delivery) |
| Windshear / Assessment | Organizational assessment engine |
| Windshear Readiness Index (WRI) | The score — one number for the board, nine dimensions for the team |
| Flightpath | Gap findings → 30/90-day remediation roadmap |
