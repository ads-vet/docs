VETINTEL03
What we learned · Act 2 — month one

The data is valuable and the culture is a superpower. Both are being held back by what we can't see.

What we learned about the data

  • The data is genuinely valuable — customers pay real money for it.
  • But they redo manual matching and cleaning before they can use it.
  • We can't see how they actually use it once it lands.
  • And they tell us they want to use it more.

What we learned about content

  • We can't measure it. We can't say which stories land, with whom, or why.
  • No story-level click-through; segments are advertiser-specific and opaque.
  • That blindness weakens ad sales and gives contributors no reason to write.
🔥 The culture is fantastic. The team jumps in, absorbs last-minute edits, and gets the issue out on time — every single week. The fix isn't more hustle; it's giving that hustle the leverage and the visibility it deserves.
