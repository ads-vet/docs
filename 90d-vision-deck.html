VETINTEL04
What we learned · Act 2 — month one

The culture is a superpower — and it's spending itself on manual work we can automate.

The culture is fantastic

Jump in, get it done, ship the issue on time — every time. Helena hand-reviewed 9,000+ shelter records, one by one, over about a month. Heroic — and exactly the kind of work we can make faster.

It shows up as heavy manual work + email

Sourcing, assembling, formatting, coordinating — all by hand. Little time left for strategic work.

On data: value trapped behind cleanup

The data is valuable, but customers redo manual matching and cleaning before they can use it.

On content: almost no traffic data

Click-through to the PDF runs about 1% of opens; segments are advertiser-specific and opaque; the product is a PDF with email as an afterthought.

VETINTEL06
Pillar 1 · Data · the model

Every EPD / IPD customer gets the platform — we monetize what they do next.

EPD = Enterprise Practice Database (~9,700 corporate-owned locations). IPD = Independent Practice Database (~19,000 independent practices).

Access for everyone

Every existing EPD/IPD member gets platform access. Still want only the CSV? You get it — exported from the same platform, same schedule.

They work in it

Inside the platform they generate reports, build and export lists, and ask questions (AI credits included under a generous soft limit) — and we surface upsell as they work.

Renewal is the moment

Every renewal call becomes a platform demo and an upsell conversation — not a CSV hand-off.
