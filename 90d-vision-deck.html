VETINTELInternal · Team Vision
What we learned · from the team and from customers

"You've got a horse that can run here. A thoroughbred — and we're treating it like a mule."

Andrew Chisholm, Phibro · July 8

From the team

  • The culture is fantastic — the team jumps in, absorbs last-minute edits, and ships every issue on time.
  • But it manifests as heavy manual work; there's little time for the strategic work.
  • On content, we can't measure it — we can't see which stories land, with whom, or why.

From customers

  • The data is valuable — but they redo manual matching and cleaning before they can use it.
  • They want it in one place, updated regularly, connected to their systems.
  • We can't see how they use it — and they tell us they want to use it more.

VETINTEL08
Content plan · automate, personalize, integrate

Automate the manual work, then pull content into the platform.

Automate

The Newsfeed Intake Agent (already built) scans hundreds of sources daily and scores candidates against our 551-story archive. Add Bitly tracking; map and remove the manual retrieval and formatting.

Personalize

Personalized feeds by role, account, company, and interest — content as its own dataset, tracking all EPD/IPD-related news.

Integrate

Prepare content so it flows straight into the platform; streamline ad proposals from resonance data.

Guardrail: the publication schedule is untouchable. Automation removes retrieval and formatting — never editorial judgment. Owners: Mark · Laura (editorial) · Graeme (audience) · Monica (placement) · Connor (ad sales) · Adam.
