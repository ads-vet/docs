The data engine · from a static file to a living platform

The data changes constantly — that's exactly why it's valuable.

Of the ~30,000 practices we track, roughly 20,000 change every quarter. Fresh, connected data is the product — not the CSV.

Appendix How we got here · why now

Built one piece at a time — now the moment is right.

How it was built

A trusted publication came first. Then ads, to support it. Then a database, because the industry needed it and the other lines couldn't fund everything alone. Each piece was added on its own — and it made sense at the time.

Why now

The market changed: more competitors (everyone launched a newsletter), cloud and AI put data work within reach, and customers expect more. We're still the trusted source — and technology now lets us sprint past, not just keep ahead.

The pieces were always meant to support each other. This is the quarter we finally wire them together.
